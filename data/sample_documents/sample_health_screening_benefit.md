# Annual Health Screening Benefit Guidelines (HSB-2026)

## 1. Eligibility for Screening
Annual health screening is available to all permanent employees.
* **Special Rule:** Employees over the age of 40 are eligible for an advanced cardiovascular screening panel.
* **Frequency:** The screening benefit can be claimed once per calendar year.

## 2. Cost Coverage
The company covers 100% of the health screening cost if completed at our partner clinic, CareFirst Medical.
If completed at an out-of-network clinic, the company reimburses up to $250.
* **Exclusions:** Experimental procedures, cosmetic laboratory tests, and DNA sequencing are excluded from coverage.
