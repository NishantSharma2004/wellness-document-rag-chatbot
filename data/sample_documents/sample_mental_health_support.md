# Mental Health and Well-being Support Policy (MHS-2026)

## 1. Counselling Services
To support employee mental health, the company provides access to professional counselling.
* **Benefit:** The company covers 6 confidential counselling sessions per calendar year.
* **Provider:** All sessions are conducted by our partner network, MindCare Solutions.

## 2. Emergency Crisis Support
In the event of a mental health emergency, employees should not use this chatbot. 
Instead, call the 24/7 Crisis Hotline directly at 1-800-555-WELL.

## 3. Fitness Reimbursement Conflict Note
*Note: For wellness-oriented mental health programs, some teams might reference a fitness benefit. Under Section 4 of this handbook, the fitness reimbursement limit is stated as $500 per year.*
*(This creates a conflict with the Wellness Policy WLP-2026 which states the limit is $300).*
