# Employee Wellness Leave and Benefits Policy (WLP-2026)

## 1. Wellness Leave Eligibility
All full-time active employees are eligible for Wellness Leave. 
Part-time employees working less than 30 hours per week and contractors are not eligible.
Eligible employees receive 5 days of paid Wellness Leave per calendar year. 

## 2. Fitness Reimbursement
The policy allows reimbursement for approved fitness activities (e.g., gym memberships).
* **Limit:** Employees can claim up to $300 per calendar year.
* **Exclusions:** Home gym equipment, clothing, and sports shoes are excluded from reimbursement.

## 3. Policy Exception
Exceptions to these rules require written approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO).
No exceptions are allowed for contractors.
